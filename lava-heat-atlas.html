One heat scale

Each vent gets a heat score from 0 to 10, built from voice loudness, pitch range, speaking pace and wording. We show it as lava temperature, so the color of a mark matches how hot the moment was.

600°750°900°1,050°1,200 °C
dull redcherryorangeyellowwhite
annoyedfrustratedfuriousraging

Under 760° lava forms a crust. That is the calm line: below it, a topic has cooled.

Magma Chamber

What are you angry about, and what's underneath it?

The anger idea

Anger is usually guarding something softer. Under Work it's feeling undervalued; under Commute it's feeling powerless. People say these words while venting, so Lava can show them back.

Tap a topic and the rest of the chamber cools to dark rock while that one swells and shows its feeling. Topics raised in the same vent melt into each other. Commute floats alone.

Ember Cards

What is each topic's temperament?

TemperamentsHover · tap to flip
The anger idea

Topics differ in how they burn, as well as how hot. Volcanologists name lava by how it moves, so we can too. Commute is spatter. Self is pillow lava: it forms under water, held in.

The delight

Hover and a card heats up to its peak temperature. Tap and it flips to show its lava type and which Lava agent cools it best.

Pressure Tubes

What's building up that you haven't said out loud?

Pressure · unventedHold a tube
The anger idea

Anger you don't express doesn't disappear. It builds. Passing mentions without a full vent add pressure, and the dashed line is this person's own tipping point.

The delight

Press and hold a tube to vent it. Steam escapes, the level drops, and Lava suggests an agent to talk it through with. Let go and it slowly refills to where it really is.

If you build two first

Magma Chamber and Ember Cards. They need only topic, heat and feeling words, they work after about ten vents, and they answer the first questions people ask: what makes me angry, how angry, and what's really going on.

What each vent needs to record

  • Topics and trigger, from the transcript
  • Feeling words under the anger: undervalued, unheard, powerless
  • Heat score 0–10, from loudness, pitch range, pace and wording
  • Start time, length and which agent listened
  • Passing mentions of a topic without a full vent, for pressure
